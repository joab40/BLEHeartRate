This Privacy Policy describes how the BLEHeartRate app (“the App”) handles information.

## Summary (plain language)

- The App uses Bluetooth to connect to a heart rate sensor and show live heart rate.
- By default, the App processes data **on-device**.
- The App does **not** sell your data and does **not** use data for advertising.
- The App does **not** use data for tracking across apps or websites.

If you later add features like cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, or sharing, this policy must be updated.

---

## What data the App processes

### Heart rate data (from a BLE sensor)
**Purpose:** Display live heart rate during training.  
**Where processed:** On your iPad (on-device).  
**Storage:** By default, heart rate is **not stored** after you close the App (unless you implement saving/export features).

### Bluetooth identifiers / device information
To connect to a sensor, the App may see device-related metadata such as the sensor name and Bluetooth identifiers used for connection.

**Purpose:** Discover, connect, and maintain a BLE connection to your sensor.  
**Where processed:** On-device.  
**Storage:** The App may store the last selected device locally to make reconnecting easier (implementation dependent). No cloud storage by default.
